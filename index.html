01 — WHY TOVI

Your phone is a tool.
Your time is yours.

TOVI is being built to help you set boundaries around the apps that compete for your attention—without guilt, noise or judgement.

05 — OUR APPROACH

Helpful by design.
Respectful by default.

Digital wellbeing should give you more agency, not another thing demanding your attention.

01

Your data stays yours.

Local-first by default. TOVI is designed to work without reading messages, photos, screen text or keystrokes.

02

No guilt. No games.

No shame-driven streaks, coins, social feeds or constant notifications. Your time isn't a score.

03

Honest about limits.

Android can interrupt background services. TOVI will show uncertainty rather than promise unbreakable blocking or perfect tracking.

TOVI is being developed for Android first. iOS support is a separate future investigation, subject to Apple's Screen Time capabilities.

05 — PRIVACY & CONTROL

YOUR DEVICE. YOUR DECISIONS.

Personal time
stays personal.

TOVI's current usage tracking works on your device. It detects foreground changes for apps you choose and records their usage locally.

  • ✓ You choose which apps are monitored.
  • ✓ Usage is recorded locally on your device.
  • ✓ TOVI does not inspect messages, photos, screen text or keystrokes.

TOVI requires you to enable its Accessibility service manually in Android Settings. Review the permissions and privacy information before installing a build.

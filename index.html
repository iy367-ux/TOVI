01 — WHY TOVI

Your phone is a tool.
Your time is yours.

TOVI is being built to help you set boundaries around the apps that compete for your attention—without guilt, noise or judgement.

02 — THE SYMBOL

A mark with
a meaning.

The TOVI symbol represents a more intentional relationship with time and technology.

BalanceMaking room for life beyond the screen.

FocusChoosing where your attention goes.

ControlPutting your time back in your hands.

03 — THE IDEA

Be intentional.
Not perfect.

Three principles guide what we're making.

01

You set the limits.

Choose the apps and daily allowance that make sense for you.

02

Every minute matters.

A simple token model is designed to make your app time tangible.

03

Pause at the right time.

Milestone reminders are planned to help you check in, not nag you.

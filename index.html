01 / THE APPROACH

Not another voice
telling you to log off.

Digital wellbeing isn't a competition to spend the fewest minutes on your phone. It's about knowing where your time goes, deciding what deserves it, and having the tools to follow through.

TOVI is being developed around that simple idea: your attention should be yours to direct.

02 / THE PRINCIPLES

Small decisions.
More deliberate days.

Three ideas shape the product. The app is still in development; these describe its intended experience, not features already available.

01

Choose your boundaries.

You decide which apps deserve limits. Your plan should reflect your priorities, not someone else's idea of a perfect day.

↗
02

Make time visible.

A daily allowance and a minute-based token model are being designed to make app time easier to understand, one minute at a time.

◷
03

Keep the choice yours.

Milestone reminders are intended to give you a useful moment to pause—without turning your phone into another source of noise.

01

Purposeful Sessions

Decide what you came to do and how long you want to spend. When time is up, take a moment to choose whether to finish, extend or leave.

02 / THE MEANING BEHIND THE MARK

A mark with
a meaning.

The TOVI symbol represents a more intentional relationship with time and technology. Its connected forms reflect three ideas at the heart of a healthier relationship with your screen.

01

Balance

Two connected, opposing forms represent balance between technology and everyday life.

02

Focus

The central structure represents bringing your attention back to what matters.

03

Control

One continuous, connected shape represents ownership of your time and choices.

03 / OUR APPROACH

Helpful, never
heavy-handed.

Your data stays yours.

Designed around on-device use. TOVI does not need to read messages, photos, screen text or keystrokes.

No guilt. No games.

No shame-driven streaks, coins, social feeds or constant notifications. Your time is not a score.

Honest about limits.

Android can interrupt background services. TOVI will show uncertainty rather than promise unbreakable blocking or perfect tracking.

Android first. iOS is a future consideration, subject to Apple's Screen Time capabilities.

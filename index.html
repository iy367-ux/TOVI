A MORE INTENTIONAL WAY TO USE YOUR PHONE

Time, on
your terms.

TOVI helps you make deliberate choices about the apps that compete for your attention — without guilt, noise or judgement.

Get to know TOVI ↓

01 / WHAT IT DOES

Make room for
what matters.

Seven connected ideas for a healthier relationship with your screen. These features are in development; details may evolve as they are built and tested.

01

Purposeful Sessions

Decide what you came to do and how long you want to spend. When time is up, take a moment to choose whether to finish, extend or leave.

INTENTION
02

TOVI Time Wallet

One daily allowance shared across the apps you select. Your time comes from the same pool, instead of separate limits that quietly add up.

TIME
03

Scroll Interruption

Gentle, configurable check-ins during long sessions. TOVI doesn't need to read what's on your screen to help you pause.

AWARENESS
04

Personal Return Cues

Leave yourself a reminder in your own words, alongside a list of things you'd rather give your attention to.

YOUR VOICE
05

Adaptive Protection

Choose Gentle, Balanced or Strict boundaries. Use schedules, a considered cool-down and an emergency bypass when needed.

CONTROL
06

Insights

Understand when you use apps, which intentions help you stay on track and what patterns are changing — with one useful suggestion, not a wall of charts.

UNDERSTANDING
07

Protection Health

See whether permissions are enabled and monitoring is responsive. TOVI will be honest about interruptions instead of promising perfect tracking.

TRANSPARENCY

02 / OUR APPROACH

Helpful, never
heavy-handed.

Your data stays yours.

Designed around on-device use. TOVI does not need to read messages, photos, screen text or keystrokes.

No guilt. No games.

No shame-driven streaks, coins, social feeds or constant notifications. Your time is not a score.

Honest about limits.

Android can interrupt background services. TOVI will show uncertainty rather than promise unbreakable blocking or perfect tracking.

Android first. iOS is a future consideration, subject to Apple's Screen Time capabilities.

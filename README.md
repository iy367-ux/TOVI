# TOVI

**Time, on your terms.**

TOVI is a digital-wellbeing product in development. We're exploring a more thoughtful relationship with everyday technology — with less noise, more intention, and more room for life beyond the screen.

The Android experience is being developed first. Product details, release timing and availability will be shared when they are ready.

## Follow TOVI

- **Website:** https://iy367-ux.github.io/TOVI/
- **Official Android releases:** https://github.com/iy367-ux/TOVI/releases

There are no official stable downloads yet. Only builds published through the Releases page should be treated as official releases.

## Development

TOVI is actively being developed. This repository contains project materials and development source; interfaces, capabilities and availability may change before release.

For product updates, use the official website. Detailed product plans and unreleased concepts are intentionally not documented here.

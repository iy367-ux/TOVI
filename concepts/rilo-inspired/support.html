HELP & GUIDANCE

We're here to help.

Questions about TOVI? Find answers to common setup and app questions below. TOVI is actively being developed, so some behaviour may vary between development builds and Android devices.

What is TOVI?

TOVI is an Android digital-wellbeing app designed to help you set intentional daily allowances for selected apps, understand your usage and pause before falling into an endless scroll.

How does TOVI track app usage?

TOVI uses an Accessibility service that you enable yourself in Android Settings. It detects when a selected app comes to the foreground and records how long it remains there. The current implementation stores its usage ledger locally on your device.

Why does TOVI need Accessibility access?

Android requires a user-enabled Accessibility service for TOVI's current foreground-app detection and intervention experience. TOVI uses it to identify selected apps and show its pause experience. It is not designed to read screen text, messages, photos or keystrokes. You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings; doing so stops this monitoring and intervention feature.

How do I choose which apps TOVI monitors?

Open TOVI's app-selection area and choose the apps you want counted toward your daily allowance. Only the apps you select are included in TOVI's allowance tracking.

What happens when I reach my allowance?

When the allowance is exhausted, TOVI's current implementation pauses selected apps until the next local-day reset. Android device manufacturers can handle background services and overlays differently, so behaviour still needs validation across devices.

Can I change the pause duration?

The current intervention supports a configurable pause of 3, 5 or 10 seconds before the intentional continuation option. The pause screen itself is not counted as selected-app usage.

Why isn't usage tracking working?

First, check that TOVI still has Accessibility access enabled in Android Settings. Make sure the apps you want to track are selected in TOVI, then reopen the app. Some Android versions apply battery or background restrictions that can interrupt services; the exact settings vary by device manufacturer. If the problem continues, include your device model, Android version and TOVI build when reporting it.

Does TOVI send my app activity to a server?

The current development implementation described in this project stores the usage ledger locally on your device. This page does not make a blanket claim about every future feature, website interaction or third-party service. Please consult the Privacy page for the current scope and check the privacy notice accompanying any future release.

How do I reset my daily allowance?

TOVI's usage allowance follows its local-day reset. The development build's reset and tracking behaviour may change as the app is tested and refined.

Where can I report a bug or request a feature?

Use the TOVI GitHub Issues page. Please avoid including private messages, screenshots containing personal information or other sensitive data in a public issue.

Still need a hand?

Open a support issue on GitHub. Share the device model, Android version, app build and steps to reproduce the problem — but leave out personal or sensitive information.

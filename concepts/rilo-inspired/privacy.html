YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE

Privacy, plainly.

TOVI is being built to help you understand and manage your selected-app usage. This page explains the data practices of the current Android development build, not a promise about features that may be added later.

Concept privacy notice · Review before public release
Important: This is a draft for the website concept. Before TOVI is publicly distributed, the project must verify this notice against the exact release build, its dependencies, permissions, diagnostics and any third-party services.

1. Information TOVI uses

The current Android implementation uses the app selections and allowance settings you configure, along with foreground-use timing for the apps you select. It uses this information to calculate your remaining allowance, maintain a local usage history and decide when to show its pause experience.

2. Accessibility access

TOVI asks you to enable its Accessibility service manually in Android Settings. In the current implementation, the service detects foreground-app changes for apps you selected and records how long they remain in the foreground. It is used to power usage tracking and the intervention experience.

The service is not designed to inspect screen text, messages, photos or keystrokes. You can disable the service through Android Settings. If you do, foreground monitoring and related intervention features will no longer work.

3. Where usage information is stored

The current development implementation stores its usage ledger and related app settings locally on your Android device. The project documentation describes this local storage model. This draft does not claim that all future versions, optional services or website features will operate without network transmission; any such feature must be explained before release.

4. Voice, microphone and camera

The current TOVI functionality described in the project documentation does not require voice recording, microphone access or camera access. If these capabilities are introduced in a future version, this notice must be updated to explain when they operate, what is processed and whether anything leaves your device.

5. Sharing and third-party services

This concept website links to GitHub for source code, issue reporting and releases. Those services are operated separately and have their own privacy practices. This draft does not assert that the website or future distribution flow has no analytics, hosting logs, cookies or third-party processing; those must be checked and disclosed accurately before launch.

6. Your choices

  • Choose which apps TOVI monitors.
  • Change your allowance and app settings within the app.
  • Disable TOVI's Accessibility service in Android Settings.
  • Uninstall TOVI to remove the app and its app-private local data, subject to Android's normal behaviour and any device backups.

7. Children's privacy

TOVI's intended audience and age guidance must be confirmed before public release. This draft does not set a minimum age or make a legal determination about children's data.

8. Changes to this notice

This notice will be revised when the app's data practices change and before a public release. The published policy should include an effective date and a way to contact the project about privacy questions.

9. Contact

For now, privacy questions and project feedback can be raised through the TOVI GitHub Issues page. Do not post personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
